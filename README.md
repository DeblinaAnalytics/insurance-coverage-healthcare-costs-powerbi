Healthcare Insurance & Treatment Cost Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

Project Overview
This project analyses healthcare treatment costs, insurance coverage, and patient outcomes using a dataset of 20,000 patient records from India. The objective is to understand how insurance status influences treatment costs, mortality, and healthcare accessibility across different demographic groups.
An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to identify trends in healthcare expenditure, insurance penetration, and regional disparities.
________________________________________
Objectives
•	Cleaning and transforming healthcare data using Power Query.
•	Building an interactive Power BI dashboard.
•	Analysing treatment costs across insurance categories.
•	Comparing mortality rates among different insurance types.
•	Studying healthcare accessibility across income levels and regions.
•	Generating business insights using DAX measures and visual analytics.

Key Insights
•	Uninsured patients incur significantly higher treatment costs than insured patients.
•	Mortality rates remain relatively consistent across insurance categories over time.
•	Insurance coverage gaps exist across all income groups.
•	Similar insurance disparities are observed in rural, urban, and semi-urban populations.
•	Insurance accessibility appears to be influenced by factors beyond income alone.
